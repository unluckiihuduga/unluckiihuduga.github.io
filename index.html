03Interactive note: why gyro bias matters

A gyroscope reports angular rate; heading comes from integrating it, so a small constant bias grows into a large heading error. When the device is known to be stationary, the true rate is zero, so the reading itself reveals the bias. A Kalman filter can estimate it from those intervals and subtract it, which is the same idea behind zero-velocity updates.
